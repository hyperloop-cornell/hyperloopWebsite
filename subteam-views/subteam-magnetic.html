Magnetics

The Magnetics subteam encompasses magnetic levitation, propulsion via a linear induction motor, and a passive horizontal guidance system. Our controllable electromagnets — built from modified transformers — work with a control system to vary supply voltage and achieve stable levitation. The LIM produces a three-phase moving magnetic pole that simultaneously attracts and repels an aluminum I-beam track to drive the pod forward.

The Magnetics subteam tackles the two defining physics challenges of hyperloop: levitation and propulsion. We design passive Halbach array levitation systems that lift the pod off the track using permanent magnets, and linear induction motors (LIM) that accelerate it electromagnetically.

Our research spans electromagnetic simulation, custom coil winding, and precision gap control. We iterate rapidly between simulation and physical bench testing to validate our models and push performance boundaries.
