The Structures subteam is responsible for the overall structural design of the Hyperloop pod. We design the chassis, built primarily from aluminum, with steel and carbon fiber where needed, develop enclosures for high and low voltage electronic systems, and fabricate the aerodynamic aeroshell.

We work closely with the Electrical teams to ensure all electronics are mounted and secured properly, promoting both safety and functionality. Every structural component undergoes rigorous FEA in ANSYS before fabrication, validated against physical load testing.
