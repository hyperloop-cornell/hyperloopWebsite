Braking

Developing pneumatic and electromagnetic braking systems for the pod — leveraging eddy-current braking at high speeds and pneumatic brakes at low speeds, with fail-safe friction actuation and a path toward regenerative braking.

Braking

Zero Contact.
Zero Compromise.

The Braking subteam designs and validates the systems that keep our pod — and everyone around it — safe. We develop dual-redundant braking architectures combining eddy-current magnetic braking with mechanical friction backup, ensuring the pod can stop under any failure condition.

Our designs are governed by competition safety regulations requiring full deceleration within defined track lengths. Every system is fail-safe by default: springs engage the brakes unless actively held open by the control system.
