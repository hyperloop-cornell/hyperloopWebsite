Power Systems

High Voltage.
Zero Compromise.

Power Systems designs and builds the energy backbone of the pod. From custom lithium-ion battery packs to the power distribution boards that route energy to motors, sensors, and control systems — we ensure every watt is delivered safely and efficiently.

Safety is paramount. Our battery management system (BMS) monitors cell voltages, temperatures, and currents in real time, triggering automatic shutdowns if any parameter exceeds safe limits. All high-voltage wiring follows strict isolation and fusing standards.
